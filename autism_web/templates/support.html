Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a medical diagnosis?
No. AutiScan is a screening tool designed to identify potential behavioral traits associated with the autism spectrum. It is not a substitute for a professional diagnosis. We strongly recommend consulting with a healthcare specialist for a formal assessment.
How accurate is the AI model?
Our model is trained on clinically validated datasets (AQ-10) and achieves high accuracy in identifying traits. However, like all screening tools, false positives or negatives can occur. Use the results as a guide, not a final answer.
Is my data kept private?
Yes. We take privacy seriously. Your assessment data is encrypted and stored securely. Only you and authorized administrators (for technical support) have access to your account details. We do not sell your data to third parties.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes. You can request full data deletion by contacting our support team via email. We will process your request within 48 hours.
